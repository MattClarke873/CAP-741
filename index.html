About

Experience entries are typed into the CAA's own CAP 741 Section 3.1 pages (Issue 4); supervisors sign and stamp in ink and you sign each page. The Work log is your own post-licence record and exports as a spreadsheet. Data stays on this phone and in your own Dropbox.
